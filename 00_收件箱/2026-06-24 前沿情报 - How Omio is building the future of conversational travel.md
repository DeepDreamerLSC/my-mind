## 中文摘要

当世界领先的旅行平台之一将实时交通数据与人工智能相结合时会发生什么？ 在这个客户故事中，Omio 分享了如何使用 ChatGPT、Codex 和 OpenAI API 重新构想旅行者如何发现和预订旅程，同时改变团队在整个业务中构建和运营的方式。 聆听 Omio 首席技术官 Tomas Vocetka 的演讲，他讨论了从基于搜索的体验到对话式旅行的转变、该公司成为 AI 原生的历程，以及 AI 如何帮助大规模加速产品开发、实验和创新。 阅读全文：www.OpenAI.com/customer-stories/omio

## 原文摘录

What happens when one of the world's leading travel platforms combines real-time transportation data with AI?

In this customer story, Omio shares how it's using ChatGPT, Codex, and the OpenAI API to reimagine how travelers discover and book journeys, while transforming how teams build and operate across the business.

Hear from Tomas Vocetka, CTO at Omio, as he discusses the shift from search-based experiences to conversational travel, the company's journey to becoming AI-native, and how AI is helping accelerate product development, experimentation, and innovation at scale.
